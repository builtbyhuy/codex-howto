# Verification and limits

**Source package: version 2.6.** The bounded release review was recorded 2026-10-23T11:58:35.823Z. A built directory does not itself prove deployment, accessibility certification, design equality or learning effectiveness.

## What is checked

The local verification process covers preserved lesson IDs/source keys and answers; progress persistence, import/export and blocked storage; failed/slow content recovery; search and lesson destinations; practice reference outputs; keyboard/dialog behavior; reflow and text enlargement; rapid interactions and reduced motion. Deliberately broken temporary copies and valid controls check whether the process detects important faults without rejecting valid cases.

Accessibility scans retain both violations and incomplete results. Incomplete items require contextual inspection, including visible targets, overlap, focus and contrast; zero automated violations alone is not WCAG certification. Visual and motion observations are scoped AI reviews, not independent human research.

The export checker independently verifies checksums, the sixty-topic contract, active presentation resources, five introduction guides, 61 static reading pages, local links/imports and exclusion of private logs. The seven fictional practice packages have independent expected outputs. Deliberately failing learner baselines are teaching material; the corresponding reference solutions are checked separately.

## What remains unproven

- No real learner trial has established first-time comprehension, transfer or recall. Completion and assessment results are self-checks, not qualifications.
- Chrome viewport emulation does not establish behavior on physical iOS/Android devices or every browser. Screen-reader usability still needs representative human testing.
- Neither visual parity with Claude How-To nor superiority to it is established.
- Five translated introductions do not mean five translated courses. The 60 detailed lessons, quizzes and practice source are in English; native-speaker acceptance of all translations is not claimed.
- Quick 10-question and full 60-question assessments use the existing course bank and link a revision plan. They do not prove practical skill or run repository-only tutoring workflows.
- Native Codex behavior can vary by client, version, account and policy. Product sources were reviewed on 21 October 2026; authenticated model tasks and cross-client execution are not implied by static or synthetic fixture tests.

Progress and quiz answers stay in browser storage. Export a backup in Sources before clearing it. No learner account or analytics endpoint is included. The workbench is an illustration; it does not execute commands or inspect your files.
